Privacy Policy

Appetina Catering & Food Services · Iligan City, Philippines
Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information we collect through our online booking system, client portal, and related pages (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep this in plain language because that is how privacy policies should be written.

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account or book an event, we collect:

  • Identity & contact details — your name, email address, and mobile number.
  • Event details — event type, date, time, venue address (including a map pin/coordinates if you choose one), guest count, chosen menu and packages, dietary notes, and special requests.
  • Payment records — amounts paid, remaining balances, and payment method (e.g. cash, GCash, bank transfer). We do not collect or store card numbers, bank credentials, or e-wallet PINs.
  • Feedback — ratings and reviews you choose to submit.
  • Account credentials — if you register manually, your password is stored only as a salted cryptographic hash; we cannot read it.

2. Sign-In with Google or Facebook

If you sign in using Google or Facebook, we receive only your name, email address, and account identifier from that provider, solely to create and sign you into your Appetina account. We do not receive your password, friends list, posts, photos, or any other data, and we never post on your behalf.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, confirm, and manage your event bookings and payments.
  • To contact you about your event — confirmations, reminders (e.g. a reminder shortly before your event), balance notices, and post-event feedback requests.
  • To pre-fill your details and suggest your favorite dishes when you book again.
  • To plan our operations — kitchen preparation, deliveries, and staffing for your event.
  • To improve our service using aggregated, non-identifying statistics.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your information is stored in our private business records hosted on Google's cloud infrastructure (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script), accessible only to authorized Appetina staff. Sign-in sessions use a temporary token stored on your own device.

5. Sharing

We share information only when needed to deliver your event: for example, your venue address with our delivery driver, or your guest count with our kitchen team. We may disclose information if required by law. We never share it for marketing by others.

6. Retention

We keep booking and payment records for as long as needed for legitimate business purposes, including accounting and tax obligations under Philippine law. Account details are kept while your account is active.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.

To request deletion of your data (including data received from Google or Facebook sign-in):
Email us at cyrabell@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request", using the email address on your account. We will delete your account and personal details within 15 business days and confirm by reply. Records we are legally required to keep (e.g. official payment records) are retained only as required.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The Service stores a small sign-in token and your preferences in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it. Google and Facebook may set their own cookies when you use their sign-in buttons, governed by their own policies.

9. Children

The Service is intended for adults booking catering services. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.
